Bars & Hospitality

Where people gather,
the woodwork matters.

Diamond Creek Golf Club — Octagon Bar Room — Banner Elk, NC

A bar is not furniture. It is architecture at elbow height — the most touched, most leaned on, most scrutinized surface in any room. Every joint has to hold up under years of hard use. Every finish has to survive spills, heat, and the weight of a crowd.

Chris Coffey has been building bars and fitting out restaurants for over twenty-five years. English-style pubs, Irish bars, high-end social clubs, fine dining rooms — from custom bartops and back bars to full commercial upfits, all wood, no shortcuts. His bar and hospitality work at the Diamond Creek Golf Club in Banner Elk, NC — commissioned by Wayne Huizenga — stands as the benchmark.

This is not cabinet work scaled up. This is a specialty — one that requires understanding how a bartender moves, how a patron leans, how light falls across a finish at midnight. Whether it is an octagon bar room under a timber-framed ceiling or a traditional English pub, Chris builds bars that are heavy, beautiful, and meant to last generations.

The Craftsman

Chris Coffey —
thirty years, one standard.

Chris Coffey learned carpentry the way it was meant to be learned — on the job, under the instruction of men who did not tolerate shortcuts. Over three decades, that foundation became a career defined by work at the highest end of the craft.

Among his most notable projects: serving as master carpenter and team leader for the Diamond Creek Golf Clubhouse in Banner Elk, NC — an ultra-exclusive private club commissioned by billionaire Wayne Huizenga, owner of the Miami Dolphins and Blockbuster Entertainment. Every timber joint, every detail of the interior carpentry, was executed under Chris's direct supervision.

Today, Chris operates from his property deep in the North Carolina Sandhills, where he mills his own timber and maintains a fully equipped joinery workshop. He brings that same exacting standard to every project — whether a fireplace surround in Pinehurst or a historic restoration in Southern Pines.

He does not subcontract. He does not cut corners. He does not take jobs he cannot do exceptionally.
